# Public repository data-access and privacy rules

**Private until independently cleared:** observed Hong Kong Wi-Fi BSSIDs or MAC addresses; raw survey scans tied to sites; original floor-plan imagery, vector layers, CAD or recoverable indoor geometry; private local coordinates (absolute or reversible relative), trajectories, AP placements; raw per-scan output; private trained checkpoints and derived sensitive artifacts.

Allowed initially: user-authored method text and code, aggregate non-identifying evaluation tables, and synthetic examples. Public datasets may be downloaded from official research publishers according to their licenses; their raw bytes must not automatically be redistributed. Synthetic examples are never evidence for the paper's empirical performance.

Before publication of any new file or release, inspect the full file contents and media, linked downloads, Git history, and rights. Relative coordinates and BSSID substitution alone are not adequate guarantees of de-identification. Original site-specific figures require a separate geometric/visual privacy audit. Third-party software/data rights are separate from original code licensing.
